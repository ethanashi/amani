## 6f. Calibration law generators and their skewness convention

The skew level of a calibration law is a BODY-SKEW level (the Tukey g of its generator). It equals the moment skewness for normal-tailed and t5-tailed laws; for t3-tailed skewed laws the third moment is infinite, so moment skewness is undefined and these cells validate the body-skew level only, not a moment-skewness bound. Kelly skewness = (Q90 + Q10 - 2 Q50) / (Q90 - Q10).
| tails | skew level | generator | moment skewness | Kelly skewness | tail index |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| normal | 0 | standard normal | 0 | 0.000 | all moments |
| normal | 1 | lognormal (Tukey g-and-h, h = 0), g = 0.3143 gives moment skewness 1 | 1 | 0.199 | all moments |
| t5 | 0 | Student t5, scaled to unit variance | 0 | 0.000 | 5 (moments of order < 5) |
| t5 | 1 | Tukey g-and-h, h = 1/5, g = 0.0778 gives moment skewness 1 | 1 | 0.050 | 5 (moments of order < 5) |
